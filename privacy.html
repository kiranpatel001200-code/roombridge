1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how RoomBridge ("we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use roombridge.vercel.app to find a room, list a room, browse Rentals, or contact us (together, the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you don't agree, please don't use the Service.

2. Information We Collect

If you're looking for a room, we collect your full name, email, WhatsApp/phone number, city & state, budget, room type preference, move-in date, and your status (e.g. employed, F-1 student, OPT/CPT/H-1B). Social media handles and specific needs are optional and only used to help us verify you.

If you're listing a room, we collect your full name, email, phone, the property's address/city, monthly rent, room type, lease terms, availability, amenities, and up to 5 room photos you upload. Social media handles are optional.

If you contact us, we collect your name, email, subject, and message.

We never store your payment card details. All payments are processed securely by PayPal — RoomBridge never sees or stores your card or bank information.

3. How We Use It

  • To review and verify your submission before any contact is shared.
  • To match renters and landlords and send verified direct contact details (phone + email) within 24 hours.
  • To respond to messages sent through the Contact page.
  • To publish your listing's non-personal details in the Rentals tab (see Section 5).
  • To enforce free-request limits and prevent duplicate or fraudulent submissions.
  • To improve the Service and keep it safe from scams.

4. How We Share It

We share your name, phone, and email with your verified match on the other side (renter ↔ landlord) only after both sides have been reviewed — never before. We also share data with the service providers that run the Service on our behalf: our backend spreadsheet/automation system for storing and processing submissions, Cloudinary for hosting uploaded room photos, and PayPal for payment processing. These providers only receive what they need to do their job.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party. We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or to protect the safety of our users.

5. Public Listings

When a room is listed, its city, rent, room type, amenities, availability, and photos appear publicly in the Rentals tab so renters can browse. Your name, phone number, and email are never shown publicly — they're only released to a renter after our team verifies them and you've been matched.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. To save you time, we use your browser's local storage to remember your name, email, and phone so your next form on this device is pre-filled. Our admin panel uses session storage to keep an administrator logged in during their session. You can clear either at any time from your browser's settings.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures, including HTTPS encryption in transit, to protect the information you share with us. Access to submitted data and the admin panel is password-restricted to our team. That said, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as needed to provide the Service — including matching you, resolving disputes, and keeping basic records — or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. Listings are removed from the public Rentals tab once filled, expired, or removed by request.

9. Your Rights & Choices

  • Ask us what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your information or remove your listing.
  • Opt out of future contact from us at any time.

To exercise any of these, reach us through our Contact page or at [insert privacy contact email]. Depending on your state or country, you may have additional rights (for example under the CCPA or GDPR) — we'll honor valid requests to the extent required by applicable law.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for users who are at least 18 years old, consistent with our Terms & Conditions. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has submitted information to us, contact us and we'll remove it.

11. Third-Party Links

Our Instagram and Facebook links, and our PayPal checkout, take you to third-party sites with their own privacy practices. We're not responsible for how those sites handle your information — please review their policies separately.

12. US-Based Service

RoomBridge focuses on room rentals within the United States, and the information we collect is processed and stored accordingly. If you access the Service from outside the US, you do so understanding your information will be handled as described in this Policy.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect once posted on this page, and we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continuing to use the Service after an update means you accept the revised Policy.

14. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data? Reach us at [insert privacy contact email] or through our Contact page. We reply within 24 hours.
